The Role of the Nepal Army

🛡️ National Defense

Protecting the sovereignty and territorial integrity of Nepal for nearly three centuries.

🌐 UN Peacekeeping

Among the world's largest contributors of peacekeepers, serving in conflict zones worldwide.

🚨 Disaster Response

First responders in earthquakes, floods, and landslides — saving lives when it matters most.

Unique Characteristics

  • Gurkha Legacy: World-renowned courage and loyalty since the Anglo-Nepal War era.
  • Non-Political Service: Serving under the constitution, never a tool of politics.
  • Himalayan Expertise: Masters of mountain warfare and high-altitude operations.
  • Sacrifice: "It is better to die than to be a coward" — the Gurkha motto.

Serving the Nation

2015 Earthquake

Led search, rescue, and relief operations after the devastating Gorkha earthquake.

Development Works

Building roads, schools, and hospitals in remote Himalayan regions.

International Honor

Peacekeepers awarded for bravery in UN missions across the globe.
